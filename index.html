Questions

Before you send a case.

Anything else, write it in the form and we'll answer by email.

What do I need to send?

At minimum, one before and one after intraoral photo. Anything taken during treatment makes the story richer, and phone photos work. For aligner cases, ClinCheck screenshots or a simulation video make the story stronger. Scans and X-rays are optional.

How long does it take?

3–5 working days from the moment we receive your photos.

Do I really pay after delivery?

Yes. For your first video there's no deposit. You see the finished video, and you pay once it's delivered.

How many revisions are included?

Three rounds per video. Change text, timing, music or emphasis until it looks right to you.

Which formats do I get?

A vertical 9:16 video for Reels, Stories and TikTok, ready to post.

Can you handle Invisalign and aligner cases?

Yes, they're among our favourites. Send before/after photos and your ClinCheck, and we'll show the movement and the final result clearly.

What about patient consent?

Posting a case publicly needs your patient's consent under your local rules. We work only from the photos you provide and never share them beyond your project.
